Tab bar side (vertical tabs)

Which side of the sidebar panel the tab bar sits on. Firefox decides which side of the window the whole sidebar is on; extensions cannot change that, so use Firefox's own sidebar settings for it.

Tab bar behavior

Shrink the tab bar to a thin strip of icons. Vertical tabs only.

While compact, show full tab names when you hover the strip.

Hide the tab bar completely until you hover the edge of the sidebar. Works on its own or together with compact mode.

Hover style

How the bar appears when it expands or reveals itself.

Toolbar buttons

These sit at the bottom of the tab bar (bottom to top when compact, left to right when expanded). Settings and Reload are always shown.

Back and Forward only appear when there is a page to go back or forward to.
